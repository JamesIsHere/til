# The whole success formula in two habits

Surround yourself with good people, and get used to being wrong. If you
have done both, you will be successful. Everything else is detail.

## Why these two

They compound each other. Good people are the ones who will tell you
when you are wrong, and being comfortable with being wrong is what lets
their corrections actually land. Either habit alone leaks value; together
they form a feedback loop that keeps improving you without any extra
effort on your part.

The simple lessons are not the easy ones — most people can recite both
of these and practice neither.
